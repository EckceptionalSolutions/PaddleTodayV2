# Georgia

The Georgia expansion now has 86 scored direct-gauge routes across six basins: Chattahoochee, Ocmulgee, Broad, Etowah, Toccoa, and Flint. The original seven routes are joined by 76 distinct NPS- or public-agency-documented Chattahoochee access-to-access itineraries plus three American Whitewater-documented Ocmulgee reaches: Lloyd Shoals Dam to Wise Creek, Popes Ferry to Amerson, and Amerson to Spring Street. Chattahoochee endpoints span Chattahoochee Pointe, Lower Pool/Bowmans Island, McGinnis Ferry, Rogers Bridge Park, Abbotts Bridge, Medlock Bridge, Jones Bridge, Chattahoochee River Park, Garrard Landing, Island Ford, Don White Park, Azalea Park, Overlook Park, Morgan Falls Park, Johnson Ferry, Powers Island, Whitewater Creek, and Paces Mill. The batch deliberately mixes Upper Chattahoochee whitewater, Atlanta managed-access blueway miles, middle-Georgia Ocmulgee shoals and flatwater, a Columbus whitewater park, northeast Georgia shoals, a long Etowah day, a Forest Service canoe-trail section, and a middle-Georgia fall float.

All 86 routes use direct USGS telemetry: 84 discharge series and two stage series. The connector thresholds use official NPS Chattahoochee flow guidance; the original seven and three Ocmulgee reaches use route-specific American Whitewater guidance. Thresholds are conservative planning aids, not universal safe limits. Every route has named endpoints, two coordinate-backed access points, safety hazards, camping classification, source notes, and approved same-river Wikimedia Commons context imagery.

The current pass does not publish Chattooga expert sections, the stale/strainer-heavy Etowah Hightower-to-Hwy 9 section, long Ocmulgee/Altamaha and tidal/coastal routes, or the cross-border Savannah Rapids run. Settles Bridge was intentionally excluded because current NPS closure information makes it unsuitable for scored publication. Chestatee, Coosawattee, Dog River, and Tallulah candidates were also screened out for unresolved access, stale or mixed lake-flow evidence, or event-only hydrology. Reopen those only after station-specific thresholds, lawful endpoint pairs, current hazard/access evidence, and any required coastal/tidal scoring treatment are complete.

Next research should focus on remaining lower Ocmulgee and Altamaha water-trail sections, any newly documented managed Chattahoochee access pairs, and lower Flint or Broad sections where Georgia Rivers or public agencies provide exact access chains and gauge-linked numeric guidance.
