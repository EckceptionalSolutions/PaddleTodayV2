## East Fork Carson scored route — 2026-09-16

Added `east-fork-carson-hangmans-bridge-washoe-road`, the popular 19.12-mile Hangman's Bridge (California) to Washoe Road River Access (Nevada) spring run. American Whitewater rates the reach II+(III), marks the Washoe Road take-out at mile 19.12 and Ruhenstroth Dam at mile 19.6, and says day trips are viable. NDOW confirms the lower valley includes private and Washoe Tribal lands while the river channel is public; boaters must stay off private banks and take out above the dam. NDEP identifies the Washoe-area water-side raft pullout at 38.86955, -119.69331; the public parking/bathroom access is at the end of Washoe Road roughly ¼ mile above the dam. Hangman's Bridge access is marginal and needs a parking/landing check.

The route uses direct USGS 10309000 telemetry and Dreamflows' same-reach Gardnerville bands (600 cfs min-good, 1,275–1,725 preferred, 5,000 max-good) as community planning cues only. Safety covers cold water, continuous early rapids, occasional Class III, steep eroded banks, strainers, few exits, land boundaries and the required pre-dam take-out. Camping is nearby-basecamp only: East Fork Resort campsites require advance operator permission; no on-route campsite is assumed. The gallery uses Matkatamiba's local East Fork rafting photo under CC BY-SA 4.0. Canonical geometry follows one connected USGS NHD line (794 vertices, 19.23 mi, 47-ft maximum endpoint snap); a targeted hydrography audit passes 2/2 endpoints. Nevada now has 43 public routes (42 scored, one planning). Evidence: `docs/operations/starter-evidence/nevada-east-fork-carson-2026-09.json`.
