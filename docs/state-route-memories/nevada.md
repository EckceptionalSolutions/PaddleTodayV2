## First batch qualified

Three scored Colorado River reaches now anchor the Nevada starter set:

- Hoover Dam permitted launch to Willow Beach (`colorado-river-hoover-dam-willow-beach`)
- Willow Beach to Eldorado Canyon / Nelson Landing (`colorado-river-willow-beach-eldorado-canyon`)
- Eldorado Canyon to Cottonwood Cove Marina (`colorado-river-eldorado-canyon-cottonwood-cove`)

The batch uses RiverScout's 6,800–20,300 cfs community planning band with direct USGS 09421500 telemetry and the NPS Black Canyon National Water Trail's river-mile boundaries and access rules. Willow Beach is on the Arizona side of the border, while the direct gauge, Eldorado endpoint and Cottonwood Cove are in Nevada; the routes are attributed to Nevada's Clark County corridor. The catalog now has six scored routes, adding Hoover–Eldorado, Willow–Cottonwood and Hoover–Cottonwood combinations. All twelve endpoints returned OK or review with no suspicious, failure or unknown results. The Hoover launch requires an authorized permit/outfitter, Eldorado requires a ¼-mile carry to vehicles, and NPS warns that the southbound continuation is open water exposed to wind and extreme heat. Recheck permits, access, live releases, weather and NPS restrictions before deployment.

## Second scored-first pass (2026-09-10)

Added three Truckee River Reno reaches: Mayberry Park to Wingfield Park, Wingfield to Cottonwood Park, and the full Mayberry-to-Cottonwood run. American Whitewater publishes the 10-mile I–III Reno reach and direct USGS 10348000 relationship; Reno’s Whitewater Park, the TMWA recreation map and Nevada Trail Finder document the public park access chain. A conservative 250 cfs low-water floor and 400–1,200 cfs planning band are recorded for the direct Reno gauge, with engineered features, diversion structures, cold water, strainers and urban access cautions explicit.

Route-scoped geometries are generated and the three routes remain validated-not-deployed pending endpoint audits and current park/release checks. Evidence: `docs/operations/starter-evidence/nevada-truckee.json`.
