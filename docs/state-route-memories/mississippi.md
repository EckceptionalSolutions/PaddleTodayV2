Campaign activated 2026-09-09. Initial public catalog count: 0.

## First scored pass (2026-09-10)

Qualified three Black Creek reaches in the DeSoto National Forest corridor using RiverScout’s direct 02479130 flow band and Seven Rivers Canoe Club’s named landing guide:

- `black-creek-big-creek-old-highway-49` — Big Creek concrete ramp to the Old Highway 49 primitive landing, about 5 miles.
- `black-creek-janice-cypress` — Janice to Cypress Creek Forest Service landings, about 6 miles.
- `black-creek-cypress-fairley` — Cypress Creek to Fairley Bridge public ramp, about 5.7 miles.

RiverScout’s 200–1,200 cfs band is a community planning cue tied to USGS 02479130, with 2,000 cfs treated as a caution ceiling in the starter profile. Black Creek is Class I–II but remote, sandy, strainers-prone and subject to flash rises. The catalog now has six scored routes using the three core Forest Service access points and their full-length combinations. All access is day-use or Forest Service managed; verify roads, parking, landing condition and current gauge trend before deployment.
