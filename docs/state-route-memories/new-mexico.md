## First batch qualified

The first scored batch uses the direct-gauge Rio Grande del Norte corridor: Taos Junction Bridge to Quartzite, Taos Junction Bridge to Lone Juniper, and the popular Quartzite to County Line Racecourse. BLM names these access sites and rates Orilla Verde Class II and Racecourse Class III–IV; American Whitewater supplies the 6.6-mile and 4.4-mile reach context. BoaterBeta ties the Racecourse 200–3,000 cfs planning range to USGS 08276500, with a 400–1,200 cfs float planning window used here. Three endpoints are OK and three are review because Quartzite and County Line shoreline anchors sit on mapped waterbodies away from the NHD centerline; none are suspicious, failed or unknown.

Qualified route IDs:

- `rio-grande-taos-junction-quartzite`
- `rio-grande-taos-junction-lone-juniper`
- `rio-grande-quartzite-county-line`

The catalog now has six scored Rio Grande del Norte routes, adding Taos Junction–County Line, Lone Juniper–County Line and Lone Juniper–Quartzite combinations while preserving the BLM access, permit, private-bank and Class III controls.

Recheck BLM fees, parking, private-bank and quiet-zone rules, Quartzite and County Line access, wind and storms, and live USGS 08276500 trend before deployment.
