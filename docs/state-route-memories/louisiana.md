## Research state

Initial scored screen (2026-09-10): RiverScout reports a 300–2,000 cfs community band for a page titled Bogue Chitto but cites USGS 07375085. USGS station metadata identifies 07375085 as Bogue Falaya at Folsom, while Bogue Chitto’s matching Louisiana station is 02492000 near Bush. Do not transfer the RiverScout band. RiverDates independently reads the same 02492000 gauge and reports a roughly 650–3,400 cfs middle-half context for the Franklinton through State Park to Bush corridor.
