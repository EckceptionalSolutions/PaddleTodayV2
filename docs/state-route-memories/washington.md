## Research state

First batch qualified 2026-09-10: three scored Yakima Canyon segments using the direct USGS 12484500 Umtanum gauge and BLM access sequence: Umtanum–Lmuma Creek, Lmuma Creek–Big Pines, and Big Pines–Roza. Endpoint coordinates were refined to the mapped water-entry edges and all six endpoint audits returned OK. Roza is the required takeout because the diversion dam is about one half mile downstream. Keep the batch validated-not-deployed until the production build and current BLM access, closure, flow, wind, cold-water and wood checks are complete. Next pass should screen Snoqualmie Valley and selected Skagit, Stillaguamish, Spokane or lower Columbia reaches for additional direct-gauge scored routes.

Second batch qualified 2026-09-10: three scored Snoqualmie Valley segments using direct USGS 12149000 telemetry and the documented Plum Landing–Fall City Community Park–Richter/Neal access sequence. The routes are Plum–Fall City, Fall City–Neal and Plum–Neal. American Whitewater documents the high-use Class I–II+ corridor, a low-runnable reference near 467 cfs and beginner float reports; Fall City sources document the river-entry trail below SR-202, and WDFW documents Richter. The Fall City endpoint was corrected from the park centroid to the river-entry trail after a coordinate audit; all remaining findings are expected access-edge reviews with no suspicious or failed endpoints. Keep the batch validated-not-deployed until the production build and current access, closure, flow, rain, wood, weather, cold-water and crowding checks are complete. Next pass should screen Skagit, Stillaguamish, Spokane or lower Columbia reaches for additional direct-gauge scored routes.
