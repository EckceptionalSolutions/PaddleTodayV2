Three specific candidates have source support but are not yet qualified or implemented:

- Willamette: Peoria Park to Crystal Lake Boat Ramp, 9 miles. [Trail itinerary](https://willamettewatertrail.org/itineraries/peoria-to-corvallis/) supplies launch coordinates and distance. [Corvallis](https://www.corvallisoregon.gov/parksrec/page/willamette-boat-landing) confirms the concrete ramp, shared parking and seasonal dock. The trail warns of swift current, strainers, slippery concrete and gaps in the ramp. Still needed: current obstruction review, live station relationship, independent endpoint/geometry review.
- Willamette: Michael’s Landing / North Riverfront Park to Hyak Park, 9 miles. [Trail itinerary](https://willamettewatertrail.org/itineraries/michaels-landing-to-hyak-park/) describes a rustic launch, current, rocks and woody backchannels. [Hyak manager](https://nape.bentoncountyor.gov/directory/hyak-park/) explicitly prohibits overnight parking and camping; use a day trip, despite the trail's overnight itinerary. The [take-out description](https://willamettewatertrail.org/map/hyak-park/) warns of current, riprap and immediate ramp carry. Still needed: current obstruction review and confirmation that take-out demands fit the intended cohort, plus gauge and geometry review.
- Tualatin: Jurgens Park to Tualatin Community Park, 1.7 miles from river miles 10.6 and 8.9. [Jurgens access](https://tualatinwatertrail.org/access-site/jurgens-park/) describes a long carry and steep gangway; [Community access](https://tualatinwatertrail.org/access-site/tualatin-community-park/) identifies the ramp and a shallow/fast section upstream. [City trail page](https://www.tualatinoregon.gov/recreation/tualatin-river-water-trail) confirms both accesses. Google links point to parks, not verified water-entry coordinates. Still needed: exact launch coordinates, current city project/access notices, obstruction review, supported gauge reference and geometry. The trail links OWRD station 14206956; do not mislabel it as a USGS gauge.
