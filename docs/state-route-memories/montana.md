## First batch qualified

Three scored Clark Fork day floats now anchor the Montana starter set:

- Milltown State Park to Sha-Ron Fishing Access (`clark-fork-river-milltown-sharon`)
- Sha-Ron Fishing Access to Jacobs Island Park (`clark-fork-river-sharon-jacobs-island`)
- Jacobs Island Park to Silver Park Boat Landing (`clark-fork-river-jacobs-silver-park`)

The batch uses RiverScout's 2,000–10,000 cfs community planning band with direct USGS 12340500 telemetry, and the Destination Missoula river guide's named access chain and typical two-hour, two-hour and one-hour floats. The catalog now has six scored Clark Fork starter routes, adding Milltown–Jacobs, Sha-Ron–Silver and Milltown–Silver combinations. All twelve endpoints were refined to mapped Clark Fork waterbody edges and audited with review severity because NHD reports flowline distance as informational for bank and walk-in access points; there were no unknown, suspicious or failure results. Silver Park is take-out only. Recheck the direct gauge, FWP restrictions, Milltown gate, Sha-Ron parking, Jacobs Island walk-in access and Silver Park event closures before deployment.
