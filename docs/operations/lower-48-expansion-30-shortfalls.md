# 30-route expansion shortfall log

Prepared: 2026-09-13

This log records where the 30-route target is intentionally left below target after a score-first review. A slot stays open when the next candidate cannot meet the catalog's public water-entry, safety, camping, direct-gauge/threshold, and non-duplicative reach requirements.

## Arizona — 7 slots open (23 routes)

The catalog now covers the documented Verde, Lower Salt, and Gila Box corridors. The Gila Box addition uses the BLM Old Safford Bridge and Dry Canyon public endpoints, the American Whitewater Class II+ reach and direct USGS 09442000 context. The remaining Gila River recreation pages warn of fences, trees, steep banks, or limited exits on the downstream access chain, so those reaches are not promoted as scored routes without a separate field-level access and hazard review.

Lake Havasu and the lower Colorado have public boating access, including BLM's Take Off Point, but the reviewed sources describe reservoir/river boating and boat-in camping rather than a paddling reach with a route-specific scored flow band. The available Parker-area USGS stations provide lake level or lower-river context, not a verified recreational threshold for a new Arizona reach. We therefore leave the remaining slots open rather than create lake loops or duplicate the existing Salt/Verde endpoint combinations.

Sources: [BLM Gila River Boat Put-In](https://www.blm.gov/visit/gila-river-boat-put-in), [BLM Dry Canyon Picnic Area](https://www.blm.gov/visit/dry-canyon-picnic-area), [BLM Take Off Point](https://www.blm.gov/visit/take-point), [USGS Colorado River below Parker Dam](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-09427520/).

## Mississippi — 2 slots open (28 routes)

The verified Black Creek landing chain is exhausted in every forward public endpoint pairing. Reversed pairs and additional combinations would be redundant. Pascagoula and Leaf River sources identify attractive blueways and public launches, but the reviewed material does not yet provide the route-specific scored threshold and complete access/camping package needed to publish another scored reach. Keep these as research leads for a future pass.

Sources: [MDEQ public-waterway access rule](https://www.mdeq.ms.gov/water/surface-water/public-waterways/), [Pascagoula River Blueway guide](https://msgulfcoastheritage.ms.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/BLUEWAYS_GUIDE_FINAL_2018compressed.pdf), [USGS Pascagoula River at Merrill](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-02479000/), [Seven Rivers Leaf River notes](https://www.sevenriverscanoe.com/leaf-river).

## New Mexico — 4 slots open (26 routes)

The reviewed Rio Grande, Rio Chama, San Juan, and Animas corridors now use every documented, non-duplicative public endpoint pairing that meets the publication bar. Southwest Paddler explicitly reports no other public access on the Bosque reach and warns that reaches below Velarde Dam are usually too low and have extremely limited access. BLM's remaining Ute Mountain access is a state-line/Colorado-to-New Mexico run with a steep carry and seasonal closure; the remaining Taos Box and Razorblades sections are advanced to expert whitewater and are held out of the scored starter catalog until a separate expert publication review is completed.

Sources: [BLM River Segments guide](https://www.blm.gov/sites/blm.gov/files/NM_Rio_Grande_and_Rio_Chama_River_Segments.pdf), [Southwest Paddler Bosque reach](https://southwestpaddler.com/docs/riograndenm9.html), [Southwest Paddler Ute Mountain reach](https://www.southwestpaddler.com/docs/riograndenm2.html), [BLM Rio Grande and Red River WSR](https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/new-mexico/rio-grande-wsr).
