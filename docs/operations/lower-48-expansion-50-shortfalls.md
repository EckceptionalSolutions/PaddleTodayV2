# 50-route expansion shortfall log

Prepared: 2026-09-16

This log records the current stop points for the 17-state expansion pass. A state remains below 50 when the reviewed public access sequence has no additional distinct forward pair that also has a scored flow profile, water-entry access, route-specific safety and camping guidance, evidence links, geometry and gallery support. Reversed pairs, near-identical access aliases and planning-only cards are not used to pad a state. The generated inventory has 15 states at or above 50 public routes; Nevada is at 43 (42 scored) and New Mexico at 38 (37 scored). The latest screening of both states is in `docs/operations/starter-evidence/lower-48-expansion-50-final-screen-2026-09.json`.
