# B101 — Turner Bridge Access Ramp, Kansas

The suspicious take-out on `kansas-river-eudora-turner-bridge` was reviewed against the current Friends of the Kaw Kansas River Water Trail page and Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks material. The water-trail page names Turner Bridge Access Ramp at River Mile 9.2, publishes the exact stored GPS coordinate, and describes the concrete ramp and gravel parking. KDWP independently identifies Turner Boat Ramp as public access.

The exact published coordinate is retained as a conditional access anchor. It is 462 ft from the matched Kansas River flowline and 332 ft from mapped water, which reflects the bridge and ramp facility context. Verify ramp condition, legal approach, river level, and bridge/industrial-area safety before use.
