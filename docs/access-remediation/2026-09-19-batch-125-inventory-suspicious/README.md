# B125 — remaining inventory-only suspicious endpoints

Two inventory-only suspicious endpoints were reviewed together because the public audit excludes their route records: the U.S. 278 Cossatot River access area in Arkansas and the Ghost Town Trail Heshbon access near PA 259 on Blacklick Creek, Pennsylvania.

Arkansas State Parks documents U.S. 278 Access as the Cossatot float take-out and access area. Indiana County Parks documents Heshbon trailhead parking on Route 259, while American Whitewater identifies the Ghost Town Trail put-in upstream of the bridge. Both stored coordinates remain the best area anchors; neither was moved to an inferred wet-toe point. Both are conditional because park/trailhead status, legal carry, water level, and local restrictions must be confirmed.

Both endpoints changed from suspicious to review in inventory-targeted and full audits. Public totals remain 2,046 ok, 3,933 review, 0 suspicious, 0 failure, 75 unknown. Inventory totals are 2,210 ok, 4,241 review, 53 suspicious, 51 failure, 86 unknown; the remaining 53 suspicious records are explicit withheld-route release holds. The registry now has 1,266 authoritative access matches and 0 conflicts.
