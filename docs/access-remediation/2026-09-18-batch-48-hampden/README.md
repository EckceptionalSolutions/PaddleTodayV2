# Hampden public launch-site anchor — batch 48

Two Penobscot River routes share **44.76540, -68.79690**, about **204 feet from mapped water and 462 feet from the matched river flowline**. Maine DACF's [public boat-launch inventory](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/parks/water_activities/boating/public_boat_launches/boat_sites.shtml) publishes this exact point for its Hampden site and describes a hard **50-foot by 200-foot all-tide ramp**. Maine DMR independently confirms public boat-ramp access in Hampden, while the [2024 town plan](https://www.maine.gov/dacf/municipalplanning/comp_plans/Hampden_2024.pdf) lists a marina and launch at Turtle Head Marina; those sources corroborate local public access but do not independently georeference DACF site 19280.

I kept the published coordinate and registered it as an `authoritative-access-anchor`. The official listing verifies a public launch site but does not specify whether its pin is the top of the long ramp or the water-entry toe. Satellite imagery did not resolve that toe with enough confidence to move the point. Both affected endpoints now classify as **review** and include a user-facing caveat to follow the designated public launch and confirm the carry, float, parking, and tide. This is a downgrade from suspicious based on primary-source evidence, while exact water-edge placement remains open.
