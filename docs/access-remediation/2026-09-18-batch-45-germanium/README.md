# Rum River DNR access-anchor corrections — batch 45

Three suspicious endpoint occurrences on the lower Rum River shared two public access locations. Both sites appear in Minnesota DNR's current Public Water Access Sites GIS, which supplies site coordinates missing from the earlier route review. I replaced the stale coordinates with the published access points and kept every endpoint at **review** because a facility/access point does not establish the exact wet launch or landing toe.

The two Germanium occurrences shared **45.2826032, -93.4079762**, 310.2 feet from mapped water and about 386.7 feet from the matched Rum River line. DNR record [WAS02989](https://enterprise.gisdata.mn.gov/aghost/rest/services/us_mn_state_dnr/struc_water_access_sites/MapServer/0/query?where=unique_swas_id%3D%27WAS02989%27&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=pjson) names the City of Ramsey carry-in at river mile 9.0 on river right and publishes **45.2822869164, -93.4070840207**. Its point is heads-up digitized, so I recorded it as an access-area anchor. DNR's [Rum River Map 3](https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/maps/canoe_routes/rum3.pdf) independently labels the Germanium carry-in at that river mile.

Reviewing the second route also surfaced a suspicious South County Park take-out at **45.2121606, -93.3907042**, 339 feet from the matched flowline. DNR record [WAS00289](https://enterprise.gisdata.mn.gov/aghost/rest/services/us_mn_state_dnr/struc_water_access_sites/MapServer/0/query?where=unique_swas_id%3D%27WAS00289%27&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=pjson) places Anoka County's GPS-collected trailer access at river mile 1.9 on river right and publishes **45.2138799163, -93.3893499946**. The county's current [park page](https://www.anokacountyparks.com/parks/rum-river-south) confirms boat and canoe/kayak launches. DNR Map 3 also places the park above the Rum River Dam, at river mile 1.9 versus the dam at mile 0.8.

Both DNR points now match route-scoped official controls. The targeted audits classify all four endpoints on the two routes as review, with no suspicious endpoints or source issues. The north route's other endpoint remains review because it lies 107 feet from mapped water; the two Germanium occurrences and South County endpoint match their official access anchors. No route was rejected or withheld because primary sources confirm all reviewed public accesses.
