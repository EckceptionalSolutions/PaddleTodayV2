# Willow Mill Park access-site correction — batch 46

Three public route occurrences shared a suspicious Willow Mill Park coordinate: **40.2567096, -77.0411725**, about 190 feet from mapped water and 319 feet from the matched Conodoguinet Creek flowline. Cumberland County's current [Conodoguinet Creek Water Trail guide](https://www.cumberlandcountypa.gov/DocumentCenter/View/49065/CCWT-Guide-with-Updated-Table-2024) identifies B4 as Willow Mill Park and publishes **40.2578, -77.0426**. The guide describes a concrete boat launch with an improved ramp, short carry, and shallow slope to the water. Silver Spring Township also confirms a boat launch at the rear of the park on its [Willow Mill Park facility page](https://silverspringrec.recdesk.com/Community/Facility/Detail?facilityId=17).

I replaced the three route endpoints with the County's published B4 point and registered it as an `authoritative-access-anchor`. That point is a rounded park-level marker, not a ramp-toe coordinate. It lies about **572 feet from mapped water and 682 feet from the matched flowline**; satellite imagery did not resolve the ramp through tree cover. Each affected route now remains at **review**, with directions to follow park signs to the concrete launch at the rear. This removes unsupported precision from the old pin and verifies the public site, but the exact water-entry point remains open.

The audit wording was also corrected: an official site-coordinate match now confirms the public access area, not the exact water edge. Targeted audits for all three affected routes show no suspicious endpoints or source issues.
