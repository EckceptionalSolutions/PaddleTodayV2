# Batch 80: Kelly Forks Campground Landing

## Decision

Retain `46.7167, -115.25515` as the Kelly Forks access-area coordinate and extend the existing Idaho control to both affected routes. The North Idaho Rivers reach guide identifies Kelly Forks as an access at the Kelly Creek / North Fork Clearwater confluence, with a campground on river left and a Forest Service work center on river right. American Whitewater independently identifies Kelly Forks Access as the start or finish access for the Kelly Creek and North Fork Clearwater reaches. Forest Service pages corroborate the Kelly Forks work center and river-side campground setting.

The point is a campground/work-center access anchor, not a surveyed wet-toe point. The access remains public but remote and seasonal; confirm road opening, campground status, parking, carry, and the exact landing before travel.

## Review steps

1. Froze the shared-coordinate candidate from the refreshed public suspicious queue before point-specific source review. See `selection.json` and `audit-before-public-cache-only.json`.
2. Checked the American Whitewater Kelly Forks access descriptions, which place the campground at the Kelly Creek / North Fork Clearwater confluence and identify it as the reach access.
3. Cross-checked Forest Service information for the Kelly Forks work center/cabin and its remote seasonal road access, plus the mapped campground coordinate.
4. Extended the existing route-scoped official control so the Kelly Creek occurrence uses the same verified public access anchor as the North Fork Clearwater occurrence.
5. Reran both targeted audits and refreshed the public and full-inventory audit reports and access registry.

## Outcome and follow-up

The suspicious Kelly Creek take-out moved to review; the North Fork Clearwater occurrence remains review. Both now match the shared authoritative access-area control, with complete hydrography coverage and no targeted source issues. The remaining review status reflects facility-to-water-edge uncertainty and the remote carry/landing conditions.

The public suspicious count decreased from 45 to 44, and the full-inventory suspicious count decreased from 98 to 97. The public audit covers 2,750 routes / 6,058 endpoints: 2,046 okay, 3,893 review, 44 suspicious, and 75 unknown. The full inventory covers 2,998 routes / 6,641 endpoints: 2,210 okay, 4,197 review, 97 suspicious, 51 failures, and 86 unknown. Source issues remain 271 public and 314 inventory. The registry has 4,042 canonical entries, 1,201 repeated entries, no conflicts, and 1,228 authoritative access matches.
