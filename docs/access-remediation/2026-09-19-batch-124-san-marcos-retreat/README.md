# B124 — San Marcos River Retreat take-out, Texas

The final public suspicious endpoint was reviewed against the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department leased-access record and American Whitewater’s take-out record. TPWD identifies San Marcos River Retreat as leased public access on the north bank, open for launching and taking out non-motorized boats by reservation and fee through August 31, 2027. American Whitewater independently lists it as an alternate take-out.

The stored coordinate remains the best route anchor. It is 315 ft from the connected San Marcos River network and 293 ft from mapped water, so it is retained as a conditional leased-access-area anchor rather than moved to an inferred wet-toe point. Reserve with the retreat and confirm the current lease, fee, hours, parking, carry path, river level, and restrictions before taking out.

The take-out changed from suspicious to review in the targeted audit. Full audit totals will be recorded after regeneration.
