# River Meadows access hold — batch 43

The County's current park inventory confirms River Meadows is a public river-access park and lists a boat launch. The location is still not precise enough for a route endpoint: the saved point **48.1797, -122.0837** is 468.7 ft from mapped water and 566.6 ft from the matched South Fork Stillaguamish flowline. Two public route starts share it.

The County facility page includes a general park marker at **48.1810259919157, -122.078125393669**. A refreshed hydrography check places that marker 724.5 ft from mapped water and 820.3 ft from the South Fork trace, with Jim Creek the nearest named waterway at 776.9 ft. It is a site locator, not the launch. The official 2024 park map shows the river and River Trail but does not mark or georeference the launch/carry.

The stored coordinate is therefore rejected as a verified launch point. Both dependent routes are held while preserving the County's verified access record and the original source route data. This does not assert that public access is absent. Reopen the routes when the launch/carry and legal approach can be located from County geodata, a County confirmation, or sufficiently clear georeferenced imagery.

After adding the holds, both source routes were re-audited with `--include-withheld`: each put-in remains suspicious at 566.6 ft from the matched flowline; there were no source issues. The refreshed cache-only public audit covers **2,750 routes / 6,058 endpoints**: **2,046 ok, 3,832 review, 102 suspicious, 0 failure, 78 unknown**, with 271 source issues and 118 review locations. The prior complete snapshot had 104 suspicious endpoints and 119 review locations; the two-endpoint decline here comes from withholding the two routes, not correcting the River Meadows coordinate.
