# B115 — Quartz Creek bridge / Forest Road 247, North Fork Clearwater River, Idaho

The suspicious take-out on north-fork-clearwater-washington-quartz was investigated as a possible public bridge/road access. American Whitewater describes a fair access point near the Quartz Creek confluence and an Aquarius campground near the bridge, but does not establish a dedicated public take-out at the stored coordinate. North Idaho Rivers warns that Quartz Creek is not a good take-out for inflatables. Forest Service road records establish road access, not a lawful boat landing or carry.

The route is withheld from the public audit and release inventory pending Forest Service confirmation of a public landing, legal parking/carry approach, and exact water-entry coordinate. The stored coordinate is unchanged. The full public audit therefore removes this route from the public suspicious queue; the inventory audit retains the endpoint as suspicious for follow-up.

After the hold, public audit totals are 2,046 ok, 3,926 review, 9 suspicious, 0 failure, 75 unknown. Inventory totals are 2,210 ok, 4,231 review, 63 suspicious, 51 failure, 86 unknown. The registry remains at 1,257 authoritative access matches and 0 conflicts.
