# Washington Creek Campground — batch 54

The highest-ranked remaining repeated public suspicious group was the Washington Creek Campground point at **46.70436, -115.55598**, shared by two North Fork Clearwater River routes. Before review, both occurrences were about **370 feet from the matched flowline** and **286 feet from mapped water**.

[American Whitewater's North Fork Clearwater access chain](https://www.americanwhitewater.org/content/River/view/river-detail/543/main) identifies Washington Creek Campground Access at Forest Road 247 mile 42.7 and places the public paddler access on the upstream river-right side of the campground bridge. It identifies the campground as the take-out for the Weitas-to-Washington run and the put-in for Washington Creek to Quartz Creek. The [Forest Service campground record](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/nezperceclearwater/recarea/?recid=80046) and its current [Recreation Opportunities layer](https://apps.fs.usda.gov/arcx/rest/services/EDW/EDW_RecreationOpportunities_01/MapServer/0) confirm the named public facility and river access along the campground. The Forest Service feature point is about 900 feet south of the stored route point and represents the broader campground, not a launch toe.

Satellite imagery shows the stored coordinate in the campground/approach area on river right, inland from mapped water. Tree cover obscures any precise carry or landing. I retained the route coordinate as a broad access-site anchor, replaced the weaker Mapcarta campground link with the Forest Service facility record, and clarified the endpoint label and notes. No coordinate move or geometry regeneration was justified. Both occurrences now classify as **review**, preserving the 286-foot offset and the need to verify the carry and exact landing locally. The Quartz Creek take-out on the Washington-to-Quartz route remains a separate suspicious endpoint.
