# Sullivan Park bridge-side access correction — batch 47

Two Spokane River routes shared a suspicious Sullivan Park coordinate at **47.674537, -117.197426**, about 437 feet from mapped water and 468 feet from the matched river flowline. The [Spokane Conservation District's access review](https://spokanecd.org/blogs/news/spokane-river-restored-access-points) identifies two public north-bank pedestrian and small-boat access points at Sullivan Park: one beside the bridge and another about 500 yards downstream. It also explains that boulders and fencing guide people to the access points. The City lists Spokane River access at [Sullivan Park](https://www.spokanevalleywa.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/Sullivan-Park-22), and the [Spokane River Water Trail launch list](https://www.spokaneriver.net/watertrail/launches/) includes the park as a canoe/kayak/SUP access.

I moved both route endpoints to the bridge-side north-bank shoreline entry visible in current aerial imagery: **47.672873, -117.196939**. This is an imagery-georeferenced location with roughly 50 feet of uncertainty, not a surveyed coordinate. The targeted audits now place the endpoint about **4 feet from the matched Spokane River flowline and on mapped water**; both occurrences classify as **ok**, with no source issues. Each route's access notes now direct paddlers to the bridge-side footpath and clarify that the access serves small craft and pedestrians.

The route-specific checks are [Harvard Road to Sullivan Park](targeted-harvard-sullivan.json) and [Barker Road to Sullivan Park](targeted-barker-sullivan.json). Selection, decision details, and source observations are in [selection.json](selection.json), [review.json](review.json), and [source-metadata.json](source-metadata.json).
