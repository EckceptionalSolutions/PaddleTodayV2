# Batch 62 — White Haven South Access Area

Reviewed 2026-09-18. Two Lehigh Gorge route starts shared a point that the public audit marked suspicious because it was about 305 feet from the matched river flowline. Pennsylvania DCNR publishes that exact coordinate for White Haven South Access Area; DCNR's boating page requires use of designated launch areas, and the official park map marks a hand-carry boat launch within the White Haven access area.

The coordinate was already correct for the named access area, so no coordinate move was justified. Added a route-scoped authoritative access control and clarified that its coordinate does not identify a surveyed shoreline toe. Both route starts now audit as review, with the current park signs governing the final carry to the launch.

Evidence: [DCNR White Haven access directions and GPS](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dcnr/recreation/where-to-go/state-parks/find-a-park/lehigh-gorge-state-park), [DCNR designated launch rules and trip lengths](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dcnr/recreation/where-to-go/state-parks/find-a-park/lehigh-gorge-state-park/whitewater-boating), and [official Lehigh Gorge park guide map](https://elibrary.dcnr.pa.gov/PDFProvider.ashx?PromptToSave=False&Size=9253955&ViewerMode=2&action=PDFStream&chksum=&docID=1737542&docName=LEGO_ParkGuide&nativeExt=pdf&overlay=0&revision=5).
