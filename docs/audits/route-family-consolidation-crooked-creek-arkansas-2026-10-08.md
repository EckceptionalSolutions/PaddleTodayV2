# Crooked Creek route-family review — October 8, 2026

## Finding

Six route pages cover one continuous AGFC water-trail chain from Lower Pyatt through Snow and Mark Oliver to Kelley’s Slab. AGFC documents the three individual legs as 6.7, 5.2, and 6.6 miles. Three other pages combine those legs into longer endpoint pairs. All six choices remain on the Crooked Creek hub with their distances, shuttles, camping classifications, access caveats, and hazards.

The standalone guides for Lower Pyatt–Snow, Snow–Mark Oliver, and Mark Oliver–Kelley’s Slab remain, since these match AGFC’s mapped legs. Lower Pyatt–Mark Oliver remains standalone because it had one click and 31 impressions in the reviewed report. Mark Oliver–Kelley’s Slab is the family’s clear performance leader and remains standalone. The Lower Pyatt–Kelley’s Slab (18.5 miles) and Snow–Kelley’s Slab (11.8 miles) composite pages now select their trip cards on the hub.

## Source-backed planning notes

- AGFC lists Lower Pyatt–Snow at 6.7 miles, Snow–Mark Oliver at 5.2 miles, Mark Oliver–Kelley’s Slab at 6.6 miles, and the full Lower Pyatt–Yellville water trail at 22 miles. The retained Lower Pyatt–Kelley’s Slab 18.5-mile option is the sum of the three listed legs to that intermediate take-out.
- AGFC ties the 12–12.5 ft moderate floating guidance to the Kelly Crossing gauge. It warns that the creek depends on rainfall and can rise quickly; the moderate band is not a full high-water safety range. Kelley’s Slab can be hazardous under some conditions.
- AGFC says almost all creekside property is private. It identifies primitive camping at Snow and Brooksher Preserve, and camping at Fred Berry with the center manager’s permission; the limit is one night per campsite. Brooksher has no road access.
- The hub links to direct USGS gauge 07055607 and directs paddlers to check the observation time, rainfall, and the selected card’s access notes.
