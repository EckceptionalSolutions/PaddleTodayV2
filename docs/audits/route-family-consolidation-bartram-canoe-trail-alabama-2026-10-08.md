# Bartram Canoe Trail route-family review — October 8, 2026

## Finding

The Bartram Canoe Trail family has 13 route choices within one Mobile–Tensaw Delta system. Their standalone pages have no observed Search Console clicks or impressions in the reviewed six-month period. The hub already presents the routes as distinct trip cards, with separate mileage, waterways, access notes, gauge guidance, safety information, and camping logistics. The 13 standalone URLs now lead to their exact hub cards. No trip choice or route-specific details were removed.

This consolidation keeps the choices that matter to paddlers while reducing the number of separate indexable landing pages for this one trail system. It does not treat shared endpoints as proof that routes are duplicates.

## Search Console and live-page checks

For April 28–October 5, 2026, the Performance report showed zero clicks and impressions for URLs matching `/rivers/bartram-`.

On October 8, URL Inspection showed the hub and Globe Creek route as “Discovered – currently not indexed,” with no referring page and no prior crawl. The Spoonbill–French’s Lake/Hubbard route was reported as “URL is unknown to Google.” Google’s live test of the Globe Creek URL said it was available to Google and eligible for indexing.

Production checks found current `200` responses, self canonicals, `index, follow`, and sitemap entries for the checked hub and route URLs. The Alabama state page links to Bartram routes, and the hub links to the route cards. These observations indicate that Search Console’s stored discovery state does not describe the current link and sitemap graph for those samples; they do not reveal why Google has not crawled or selected them for indexing.

## Route distinctions retained

Alabama State Lands’ brochure documents distinct paths, distances, access points, campsites, and delta navigation cautions. In particular, French’s Lake–Hubbard Landing has three different itineraries: the 6.8-mile Globe Creek day trail, an approximately 11-mile Canal Island floating-platform overnight, and a 16-mile Spoonbill Sandbar land-campsite overnight. Other trip cards cover separate loops from Hubbard Landing, Rice Creek, and Upper Bryant, plus multi-night routes between launch sites.

The consolidated cards retain route-specific details. The hub also explains that the Mobile–Tensaw Delta contains confusing channels and directs paddlers to the marked trail and current access, water, weather, and reservation information.

## Limits

Zero impressions in a small family does not prove why Google has not indexed the URLs or explain the site-wide traffic decline. The active examples passed basic fetch, canonical, robots, and sitemap checks. No sitemap submission, URL Inspection indexing request, or Search Console validation request was made.
