# Tradewater River route-family review — October 7, 2026

## Finding

The public lower-Tradewater catalog has nine trip choices from Bellville to Granger. KDFWR documents four consecutive access segments: Bellville–Montezuma (9.2 mi), Montezuma–Fishtrap (6.7 mi), Fishtrap–VFW Bridge (9.5 mi), and VFW Bridge–Granger Landing (10.6 mi). Five additional choices combine two or more of those same segments.

USGS 03384100 near Providence is the gauge reference on these routes. KDFWR rates 10–15 ft and 5–50 cfs as good there and publishes a separate Olney range for the upstream river. The Providence station is marked as proxy context on the Fishtrap–VFW, Fishtrap–Granger, and VFW–Granger choices, which remain planning-only. KDFWR warns that rain can cause the flashy Tradewater to rise quickly.

## Decision

Keep standalone pages for the four consecutive KDFWR access segments:

- `tradewater-river-bellville-montezuma`
- `tradewater-river-montezuma-fishtrap`
- `tradewater-river-fishtrap-vfw-bridge`
- `tradewater-river-vfw-bridge-granger-landing`

Consolidate the five longer endpoint combinations to their exact trip cards on `/rivers/by-river/tradewater-river/`:

- `tradewater-river-bellville-fishtrap`
- `tradewater-river-bellville-vfw-bridge`
- `tradewater-river-montezuma-vfw-bridge`
- `tradewater-river-fishtrap-granger-landing`
- `tradewater-river-montezuma-granger-landing`

All nine choices stay available with their own distance, access, and direct-versus-proxy gauge assignments. The hub now shows the source-listed section chain and the separate Providence/Olney gauge contexts.
