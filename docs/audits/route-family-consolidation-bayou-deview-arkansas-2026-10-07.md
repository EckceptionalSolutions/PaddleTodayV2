# Bayou DeView route family review — 2026-10-07

## Decision

Treat the nine Bayou DeView records as one source-backed water-trail access chain. Keep four standalone pages: the full 15.2-mile Benson Creek–Bank of Brinkley trail planner and three official short legs (Hickson Lake–Rock Island Road, Rock Island Road–Apple Lake, and Apple Lake–Bank of Brinkley). Consolidate the five longer overlapping endpoint combinations to their matching trip options on the river hub. Preserve every trip record, access point, distance, road check, seasonal closure, navigation note, and camping restriction.

## Gauge guidance correction

AGFC says a gauge height around 14 feet or higher is needed to paddle Bayou DeView, warns that high water is dangerous, and separately says the Dagmar Road gate closes at 17 feet. The route records had turned the road-gate closure into a 14-to-under-17-foot paddling band. These facts describe different conditions, so the routes now use the source-backed low-water floor with minimum-only scoring. No route-wide upper safe-water threshold is published. The 17-foot value stays in route notes as an access-road closure check.

The pages also carried hard-coded USGS readings from July 2026 in their evidence and confidence copy. Those observations are removed; the current gauge is supplied live by USGS 07077730.

## Hub guidance

The hub links the full water-trail planner and the three source-listed short sections. It explains the south-flowing route, difficult floodplain channel finding, the georeferenced map, the around-14-foot paddling floor, the separate 17-foot road-gate closure, Apple Lake's November 1–February 15 access closure, and the different Dagmar WMA / Cache River NWR camping rules.

## Source review

- [AGFC Bayou DeView Water Trail](https://www.agfc.com/things-to-do/water-trails/bayou-deview-water-trail/) publishes the access sequence, distances, southward flow, around-14-foot paddling guidance, 17-foot Dagmar Road gate closure, high-water danger, navigation requirements, Apple Lake seasonal closure, and camping boundary.
- [AGFC georeferenced trail map](https://www.agfc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/bayoudeviewwatertrail.pdf) confirms the official access coordinates, distances, public launch locations, primitive campsites, and north-to-south direction.
- [USGS 07077730 at Bayou DeView near Brinkley](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/USGS-07077730/) provides the live gauge reading.
