# Mulberry Fork route-family review — October 8, 2026

## Finding

Three route pages describe one continuous Upper Mulberry Fork access chain: Blountville Road–CR 10, CR 10–Garden City River Park, and Blountville Road–Garden City River Park. The two Blountville combinations share endpoints with the CR 10 trip and one another; their route-specific distance, intermediate access, shuttle, and caution notes remain available as selectable cards on the Mulberry Fork hub. Their standalone route URLs now point to those exact cards. The CR 10–Garden City route remains the only standalone trip guide.

This preserves all three endpoint choices while reducing the family from three route landing pages to one. American Whitewater says most Upper Mulberry trips start at CR 10 and end at the Garden City park. It also identifies the broader Blountville Road–US 31 Upper reach. Outdoor Alabama confirms the Garden City River Park kayak launch, but cautions that banks may be private and access at county-road bridges is limited; its current Mulberry Fork guide does not specifically confirm the Blountville Road or CR 10 carry and parking details. The hub now asks users to verify those details before unloading.

## Source-backed planning notes

- American Whitewater identifies the Upper Mulberry as a Class I–II reach from Blountville Road to U.S. 31, gives a direct Garden City gauge cue, and says most trips start at CR 10 and take out at Garden City River Park.
- The route cards retain the 6.5-mile CR 10–Garden City option, the 12.5-mile Blountville–CR 10 option, and the 19-mile Blountville–Garden City option. The longer Blountville choices remain contingent on current legal access, parking, and water entry at the upstream point.
- Outdoor Alabama says river-bottom and adjacent land can be privately owned, limited access can be obtained at county-road bridge rights-of-way, and the Garden City park has an improved kayak launch. Follow the access notes for each selected card.
- Use USGS 02450000 near Garden City for direct stage context. American Whitewater’s above-2-ft cue and Paddle Today’s 4.5–6-ft community target are planning references; neither guarantees safe conditions. Check observation time, rising trend, rainfall, debris, and route-specific access before launch.
