# Goose Creek route-family review — October 8, 2026

## Finding

Five selectable trips cover one continuous 7.8-mile lower-Manchester access chain in Clay County: Jacks/Bowling Branch, Tobacco Road, Dump Hollow, and Laurel Branch. KDFWR lists three consecutive legs (2.2, 0.8, and 4.8 miles); the other two choices combine adjacent legs into 3.0- and 7.8-mile trips. All five use the same Manchester gauge context and KDFWR’s same recommended level bands.

The standalone pages repeated much of the same creek, gauge, access, and safety context. The route choices remain meaningful for paddlers because they have different distances, endpoints, and shuttle plans, so the cleanup keeps all five cards and consolidates their detail URLs onto the Goose Creek hub.

## Search Console

For April 28–October 5, 2026, the Performance report filter matching `/rivers/goose-creek-` showed 16 impressions, 0 clicks, and average position 9.3. The `/rivers/by-river/goose-creek/` hub showed 5 impressions, 0 clicks, and average position 7.8. The family filter may include historical data for the two composite URLs that were consolidated earlier; these low totals are directional, not proof of why the pages were not indexed or of a site-wide traffic cause.

## Decision

Consolidate the remaining three standalone segment pages with the two previously consolidated combination pages. All five URLs now resolve to their corresponding trip cards on `/rivers/by-river/goose-creek/`. The hub explains the shared corridor and gauge, warns that KDFWR’s “Good” levels are recommendations rather than safety limits, and distinguishes the upstream Rawlings/Stinson low-head-dam warning from these trips. Each trip card exposes its own distance, endpoints, gauge context, shuttle, access caveats, and hazards.

The hub’s source guide also calls out Dump Hollow as a ford-side access with very limited parking and a high-water crossing/turn-around warning. No sitemap or URL Inspection request was submitted.
