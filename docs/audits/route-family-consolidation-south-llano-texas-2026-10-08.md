# South Llano route-family review — October 8, 2026

## Finding

Five trip cards cover two connected but distinct parts of the South Llano access chain: TPWD-listed Cupgrass to South Llano River State Park (9.3 miles), and TPWD's official 6.3-mile state-park-to-Junction paddling trail. The official trail also publishes two alternate sections: State Park–Flatrock Lane (4.7 miles) and Flatrock Lane–Junction City Park (1.6 miles). Cupgrass–Junction is a 15.6-mile combination of the 9.3-mile and 6.3-mile reaches.

The hub keeps all five trip cards and their route details. The State Park–Flatrock route page is consolidated into its exact hub card; it had no clicks and 42 impressions in the reviewed Search Console report. The longer Cupgrass–Junction composite page is also consolidated into its card because it combines already documented reaches. The full 6.3-mile official trail stays standalone (6 clicks), as does Flatrock–Junction (1 click). Cupgrass–State Park stays standalone because it is a separately documented 9.3-mile corridor with a distinct upstream launch, subject to the access-status issue below.

## Search Console

For April 28–October 5, 2026, the URL prefix `/rivers/south-llano-river-` showed 160 impressions, 7 clicks, 4.4% CTR, and average position 10.4. State Park–Junction accounted for 6 clicks and 74 impressions; Flatrock–Junction had 1 click and 30 impressions; State Park–Flatrock had 42 impressions and no clicks. The two Cupgrass URLs did not appear in the page rows returned for this family report. The South Llano hub showed no impressions in the reviewed period.

These figures are small and directional. Performance data does not establish index status or explain the site's overall traffic decline.

## Access status that needs confirmation

TPWD's [Cupgrass access page](https://tpwd.texas.gov/fishboat/fish/recreational/rivers/lease_access/south_llano_cupgrass.phtml) still describes the gated leased access, its 9.3-mile route to the state park, Bailey Creek launch, and advance parking instructions. That page lists the lease period as December 1, 2021 through August 31, 2026. However, TPWD's [leased-access program index](https://tpwd.texas.gov/fishboat/fish/recreational/rivers/lease_access/index.phtml) still includes Cupgrass. This leaves current public access ambiguous. The route pages and hub now tell paddlers to confirm the lease and launch are active with TPWD before using Cupgrass; if confirmed, they should follow current parking instructions.

## Source-backed planning notes

- TPWD lists the state-park, Flatrock Lane, and Junction City Park access points, the 6.3-mile full trail, and its 4.7-mile and 1.6-mile alternate sections. It requires paddlers to exit before the damaged bridge apron at the state park, portage at the designated location, and reenter downstream.
- TPWD describes quiet pools, riffles, and runs, warns about snags at low water and dangerous conditions after heavy rain, and instructs paddlers to respect private property outside direct hazard portages.
- The route cards link to USGS 08149900 at Flat Rock Lane near Junction. The route's 65 cfs floor is community guidance, not a TPWD limit; check the latest gauge timestamp, rain, and trend before launch.
- Hub cards retain the original endpoint choices, distances, shuttles, access caveats, and hazards after the two standalone pages are consolidated.
