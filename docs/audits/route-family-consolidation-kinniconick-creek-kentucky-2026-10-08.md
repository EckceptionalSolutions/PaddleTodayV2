# Kinniconick Creek route-family review — October 8, 2026

## Finding

Six route pages cover one continuous Kinniconick Creek access chain in Lewis County. They share the same Tygarts Creek proxy gauge, KDFWR level recommendations, and creek hazards. Three are focused trips; the other three combine sections already represented by those choices.

The hub keeps all six endpoint choices and their route-specific distance, shuttle, access, and safety details. The three longer composites now select their exact trip cards on the hub, while the Leatherwood Branch–McDowells Creek, McDowells Creek–Mill Pond Creek, and Mill Pond Creek–Garrison pages remain standalone.

## Source-backed access chain

KDFWR lists these access-to-access distances:

- Leatherwood Branch to Puncheon/Pipe Lick: 13.1 miles.
- Puncheon/Pipe Lick to McDowells Creek: 14.1 miles.
- McDowells Creek to Mill Pond Creek: 1.6 miles.
- Mill Pond Creek to Garrison Ramp: 2.7 miles.

The existing Leatherwood Branch–McDowells Creek route represents the combined 27.2-mile upper chain. The 28.8-mile Leatherwood Branch–Mill Pond, 4.3-mile McDowells Creek–Garrison, and 31.5-mile Leatherwood Branch–Garrison trips are retained as selectable options but no longer have separate landing pages.

KDFWR says there is no current stream gage on Kinniconick Creek and recommends using Tygarts Creek near Greenup because levels closely mimic the creek. Its “Good” ranges of 2.5–3.5 feet and 50–130 cfs are recommendations, not safety limits. KDFWR describes the stream as semi-shallow with rock bars, woody debris, riffles, and pools. Its general stream-fishing guidance notes that most Kentucky streams border private property. The hub directs paddlers to named public accesses and the selected card’s carry-down, parking, shuttle, and camping notes.
