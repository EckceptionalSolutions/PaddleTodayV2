# Suwannee River Wilderness Trail route-family review

Date: 2026-10-07

## Finding

The catalog contains 44 start/end combinations across the Suwannee River Wilderness Trail access chain. Most use the same White Springs gauge, scoring cues, safety background, and river-camp context while combining the same named camps and managed park landings into longer alternatives. Florida DEP's data book lists the access sequence and mileposts; Florida State Parks publishes several explicit river-camp legs and a current closure notice for Holton Creek.

The current Florida State Parks notices say Holton Creek River Camp remains closed after Hurricane Helene damage and the Suwannee River State Park campground is closed to reservations and walk-ins effective June 3, 2026. The boat-ramp and river-only landing status can differ from campground status, so verify it separately before staging. Six Peacock Slough combinations remain withheld because the camp coordinate is an access-area anchor rather than a verified exact landing. Eight additional Holton Creek endpoint combinations are withheld while its river camp is closed; the Holton-to-Peacock route is already included in the Peacock access hold.

## Decision

Keep nine source-backed standalone pages:

- Stephen Foster State Park to Woods Ferry River Camp
- Woods Ferry River Camp to Spirit of the Suwannee
- Stephen Foster State Park to Spirit of the Suwannee, the published advanced day trip
- Gibson County Park to Suwannee River State Park
- Suwannee River State Park to Dowling Park River Camp
- Dowling Park River Camp to Lafayette Blue Springs State Park
- Dowling Park River Camp to Peacock Slough River Camp
- Lafayette Blue Springs State Park to Peacock Slough River Camp
- Peacock Slough River Camp to Adams Tract River Camp

Keep all 30 currently supported trip choices available in the hub picker. Consolidate the 21 active composite route pages into their selected trip cards, and send the 14 unavailable routes to the hub's access-notice section. The source-route records and per-trip distances, access, camping notes, gauge context, and hazards remain available where the trip is currently selectable.

## Data corrections

Three route distances disagreed with the explicit Florida State Parks river-camp leg distances. Updated Woods Ferry–Spirit from 9.5 to 10.8 miles, Dowling Park–Lafayette Blue from 10.5 to 9.7 miles, and Lafayette Blue–Peacock Slough from 8.0 to 8.3 miles. The trip evidence now links to the source that publishes each selected distance. Other miles continue to follow their named source or the DEP access-point mileposts.

## Changes

- Added exact redirect pages for consolidated and unavailable route URLs. Active trip redirects select the matching route on the Suwannee hub; held routes land at the current access notices. They are generated after the static build and excluded from the sitemap; see the [redirect delivery audit](route-consolidation-redirect-delivery-2026-10-07.md).
- Added an indexable hub section for the named trail legs, current Holton and state-park campground notices, and the White Springs gauge context.
- Updated route evidence to include the official State Parks camp-leg and park-status sources. Multi-day trip notes now tell paddlers to confirm open overnight stops rather than assume Holton or the State Park campground is available.
- Added eight Holton endpoint holds and regenerated the withheld list. The route family now has 30 public choices, nine standalone route pages, 21 consolidated public trip options, and 14 access-held source records.
- Registered the river as a condition family with no continuous-route edges.

## Rollout limits

The candidate build completed with 3,143 sitemap URLs, 2,703 standalone route pages, and 105 active consolidated trip records across the four reviewed families. That is 29 fewer sitemap URLs than the Woonasquatucket candidate: 21 consolidated pages plus eight newly withheld routes. The built-page audit checked 3,081 route links and found no orphaned public pages, missing directory inlinks, duplicate route headings or descriptions, errors, or warnings. The hub includes all nine named section links, and all 35 Suwannee redirect targets match the catalog mapping. These are local candidate results; the production sitemap and Google index change only after deployment and recrawl. The closure status is based on the live Florida State Parks notices checked on October 7, 2026; review these notices before a later release so old closures are not shown after facilities reopen.
