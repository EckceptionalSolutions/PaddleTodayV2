# Upper Cumberland River route-family review — October 7, 2026

## Finding

The public catalog has 10 Upper Cumberland choices. KDFWR documents the consecutive Varilla–4 Mile (13.8 mi), 4 Mile–Artemus (9.8 mi), and Artemus–Barbourville (5.1 mi) access segments, plus Williamsburg–Redbird (11.5 mi). Three additional cards combine the first access chain into longer endpoint pairs. The route cards all use USGS 03404000 at Williamsburg, but the flow guidance differs for the upstream access chain and the downstream Cumberland gorge.

KDFWR's Upper Cumberland page recommends 3.0–3.8 ft (300–700 cfs) at Williamsburg. Its separate Cumberland River paddling guidance calls 500–1,100 cfs best for the gorge and says flows up to 2,000 cfs can challenge skilled paddlers. The Williamsburg–Long Bottom, Williamsburg–Thunderstruck, and Williamsburg–Cumberland Falls choices retain their distinct take-outs and safety context, including the mandatory take-out above the falls.

## Decision

Keep standalone pages for the three named upper access segments and the four Williamsburg-start section guides. Consolidate the three composite combinations to their exact trip cards on `/rivers/by-river/upper-cumberland-river/`:

- `upper-cumberland-river-varilla-artemus`
- `upper-cumberland-river-varilla-barbourville`
- `upper-cumberland-river-4-mile-barbourville`

All 10 choices remain in the hub selector with their endpoints and route notes. The hub now puts the published access chain beside the separate gorge flow guidance so users can distinguish the section before choosing a route.
