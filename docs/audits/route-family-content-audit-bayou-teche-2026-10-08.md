# Bayou Teche route-page content audit — October 8, 2026

## Findings

The route inventory contains 44 Bayou Teche trip options. Eleven consecutive dock-to-dock segments remain standalone route pages; 33 longer or overlapping combinations already point to their exact choices on the Bayou Teche hub. The standalone set corresponds to the named National Paddle Trail sections, so this review retained those choices.

Every option inherited the same `3–7 hours` estimate, whether the trip was a 4.6-mile short reach or a route of several dozen miles. The [TECHE Project Paddle Planner](https://www.techeproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/PaddlePlanner_spreads.pdf) publishes directional estimates for the adjacent sections and explains that paddling pace varies by section. It lists, for example, four hours from Port Barre to Leonville, two hours 45 minutes from Leonville to Arnaudville, and five hours from St. Martinville to Loreauville including the portage. Longer composite itineraries should not inherit a short-trip estimate.

The shared planning note also said “the app has no verified ... scoring range,” which described an internal implementation detail. A duplicate “Conditions posture” evidence note repeated the same gauge limitation already shown elsewhere, and the same USGS source link was listed twice.

## Changes

- Added the TECHE planner as a source and derived each itinerary's on-water estimate from its component section times. The route copy distinguishes long or staged trips and adds time for stops, wind, current, and the vehicle shuttle.
- Kept the Baldwin–Franklin and Franklin–Centerville estimates explicitly caveated: the planner's figures cover the reverse direction, and TECHE notes that flow can vary near Franklin.
- Replaced the app-centric gauge note with a direct explanation that the Port Barre station's available record does not establish a paddling threshold for these dock-to-dock reaches.
- Removed the repeated conditions evidence note and duplicate USGS source link.

These changes improve the accuracy and transparency of the trip-planning content. They do not establish why Google selected or excluded any particular URL, and they do not guarantee a ranking or indexing change. Search Console remains read-only; no sitemap submission, validation, or indexing request was made.
