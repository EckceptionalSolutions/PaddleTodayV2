- Verified fit: the Conservancy's current resource page already links river maps, parks, studies, and USGS River Gauges. Its contact page offers a message form.
- Suggested destination: [How to Read a River Gauge for Paddling](https://paddletoday.com/guides/how-to-read-a-river-gauge-for-paddling/)
