# St. Louis River route-family review — October 7, 2026

## Finding

The public catalog has eleven endpoint choices across Minnesota DNR Water Trail Maps 1 and 2. DNR identifies three recommended trips in these areas: County Road 4 to County Road 95 (7.3 miles) and Forbes to Zim (12.9 miles) on Map 1, plus Paupores to Brookston (7.9 miles) on Map 2. Those recommendations are useful starting points, but the river has materially different conditions and access across its upper and middle reaches.

Eight catalog pages describe focused access legs. Three additional pages are generated combinations of adjacent legs: County Road 95 to Zim (31.5 miles), Forbes to Toivola (31.7 miles), and Toivola to Floodwood (22.1 miles). Their endpoints remain useful itinerary choices, but the pages repeat component-route descriptions and gauge context. The cards can preserve each combination's mileage, access points, gauge assignment, and hazards without keeping three more near-duplicate indexable pages.

Gauge context must remain route-specific. The Forbes station and its interpreted bands describe the upper corridor; the Floodwood station and its different stage bands describe the middle corridor. Some routes use one station as a proxy, which should be clear on the trip card and paired with local depth checks. County Road 95–Zim crosses the Forbes Dam boundary and requires the river-right portage shown on DNR Map 1. Forbes–Zim begins at the public access downstream of the dam. Map 2 includes Class I–II rapids that DNR says can be dangerous in high water. DNR Map 3, from Highway 2 to Lake Superior, is a different downstream whitewater area and is outside this eleven-trip catalog.

## Decision

Keep standalone route pages for the eight focused options:

- `st-louis-river-county-road-4-95`
- `st-louis-river-county-road-95-forbes`
- `st-louis-river-forbes-zim`
- `st-louis-river-zim-toivola`
- `st-louis-river-toivola-county-road-29`
- `st-louis-river-county-road-29-floodwood`
- `st-louis-river-floodwood-paupores`
- `st-louis-river-paupores-brookston`

Consolidate these three composite pages to their exact trip cards on `/rivers/by-river/st-louis-river/`:

- `st-louis-river-county-road-95-zim`
- `st-louis-river-forbes-toivola`
- `st-louis-river-toivola-floodwood`

All eleven endpoint choices remain selectable on the hub. The hub guide starts with the DNR-recommended trips, explains the two map and gauge zones, preserves the three longer combinations as direct card links, and calls out the Forbes Dam portage and the separate Map 3 whitewater area. No sitemap submission or URL Inspection indexing request was made.
