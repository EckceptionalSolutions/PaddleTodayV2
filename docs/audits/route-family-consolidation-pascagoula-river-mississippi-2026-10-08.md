# Pascagoula River (Mississippi) route-family follow-up — October 8, 2026

## Decision

The Pascagoula family has 12 selectable trip options, but the route URLs had no Search Console clicks or impressions from April 28 through October 5, 2026. The river hub also had zero clicks and impressions in that period. This is a small-family observation, not an explanation for the site's wider traffic decline or proof of why Google selected or excluded individual pages.

The family needs two blueway contexts. The Mississippi Gulf Coast National Heritage Area describes the George County Blueway as a nearly 23-mile trip from Merrill to East or West Wilkerson Ferry, with strong main-river current for intermediate or experienced paddlers and permit requirements in the WMA. It describes a separate 10-mile Jackson County Blueway. The City of Pascagoula says the lower trail takes about six hours at a moderate pace and that its intermediate access points support shorter trips.

Keep those differences and all 12 trip options. Consolidate only `pascagoula-river-game-management-big-creek` to its exact hub card. Its roughly 11-mile itinerary combines the adjacent Game Management River Road–Highway 26 and Highway 26–Big Creek choices, which remain separately selectable. The broader 55-mile Highway 26–River Park plan remains distinct because it crosses from the George County access chain toward the separate lower Jackson County paddling area and needs its own planning caveats.

This reduces Pascagoula standalone landing pages from seven to six without removing an endpoint pair. The George County guide link now opens the already-consolidated 23-mile trip card directly instead of sending users through its retired route URL.

## Sources and Search Console evidence

- Mississippi Gulf Coast National Heritage Area, [George County Blueway](https://msgulfcoastheritage.ms.gov/natural/blueways/pascagoula-river-george-county-blueway/): nearly 23 miles, Merrill to East or West Wilkerson Ferry, strong current, intermediate/experienced paddlers, and WMA permits.
- Mississippi Gulf Coast National Heritage Area, [Jackson County Blueway](https://msgulfcoastheritage.ms.gov/natural/blueways/pascagoula-river-jackson-county-blueway/): separate 10-mile blueway.
- City of Pascagoula, [Outdoor Recreation and Paddling](https://cityofpascagoula.com/327/Outdoor-Recreation): approximately six hours at a moderate pace and shorter options from intermediate access points.
- Search Console Performance, April 28–October 5, 2026: route-prefix filter and exact river-hub filter each showed zero clicks and impressions.

No sitemap submission, URL Inspection indexing request, or Search Console validation request was made.
