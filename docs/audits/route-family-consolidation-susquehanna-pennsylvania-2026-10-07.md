# Susquehanna North Branch route-family audit

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Scope:** Pennsylvania North Branch Susquehanna route pages, the river hub, Google Search Console examples, and legacy URLs.

## Evidence reviewed

- Search Console’s page indexing report was last updated October 3, 2026. It showed 3.82K known URLs not indexed and 836 indexed, including 450 “Crawled—currently not indexed” and 2,171 “Discovered—currently not indexed.”
- The 450-URL crawled-not-indexed export included 14 Susquehanna route URLs in the sample. This is evidence for reviewing the family, not proof that every page has the same cause.
- For July 5–October 4, 2026, the Search Console Performance report filtered to the Susquehanna route prefix showed 11 clicks, 489 impressions, a 2.2% CTR, and an 8.4 average position across 34 URLs. The Ulster Bridge–Towanda page led with 113 impressions; Danville–Montgomery–Shikellamy North had 2 clicks and 20 impressions; PFBC Danville–Wray’s had 1 click and 16 impressions. The legacy Tunkhannock–West Falls URL had 3 clicks and 18 impressions.
- The Pennsylvania source file contains 22 North Branch route records. Two Sayre routes are held from publication because their corrected access coordinates still need regenerated route geometry. The river hub combines those Pennsylvania routes with 13 upper main-stem routes in New York, for 33 currently selectable options: 20 in the Pennsylvania North Branch family and 13 in New York.
- The PFBC and Susquehanna Greenway publish section maps and named endpoint trips. PFBC uses local gauge references at Towanda, Meshoppen, Wilkes-Barre, Bloomsburg, and Danville, so consolidation must preserve the section-specific gauge and access context.

## Page-family decision

Keep 12 source-backed pages as focused section planners or distinct day trips:

- Ulster Bridge–Towanda and Ulster Bridge–Wysox Township Park.
- Towanda–Laceyville, Laceyville–West Falls, Canal Park–Test Track, and Test Track–Danville section planners.
- Wetlands–Bloomsburg, PFBC Bloomsburg–Montgomery Park, PFBC Danville–Wray’s, PFBC Danville–Shikellamy West, Danville–Montgomery–Shikellamy North, and Riverside Borough–Shikellamy North.

Consolidate eight overlapping endpoint pages to their exact trip cards on the Susquehanna hub:

- Hornbrook–Wysox Township Park.
- Ulster Bridge–Terrytown, Hornbrook–Terrytown, Towanda–Terrytown, and Wysox Township Park–Terrytown.
- Hornbrook–Towanda, Wray’s–Shikellamy West, and Test Track–Indian Head.

The route records and all 33 currently public endpoint options remain in the hub picker. The change reduces indexable Pennsylvania North Branch route landing pages while preserving each selectable endpoint pair, its mileage, access, gauge, and hazard notes. The hub copy now distinguishes the 13 New York main-stem choices from the 20 Pennsylvania North Branch choices and their separate gauge context.

## Redirect repairs

Twelve legacy URLs that appeared as 404s in the reviewed Search Console exports now redirect to their nearest current section planner. Two additional held Sayre URLs redirect to the Susquehanna hub while their route geometry remains unpublished. Existing redirects for Canal Park–Wetlands and Test Track–Bloomsburg also lead to current guides.

## Site changes

- Added a North Branch section guide to the Susquehanna hub, grouped from Bradford County through Shikellamy, with direct PFBC and Susquehanna Greenway source links.
- Added the eight consolidated route URLs to the route-page redirect map; each redirect selects the matching retained trip card.
- Added permanent redirects for the 12 reported legacy 404s and the two withheld Sayre page URLs. Updated the Test Track–Bloomsburg redirect to land on the Test Track–Danville planner.
- No Search Console indexing request or sitemap submission was made as part of this change.
