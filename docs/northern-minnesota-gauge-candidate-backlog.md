# Northern Minnesota gauge-backed route backlog

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

## Publication rule

A route is publishable only when it has a non-empty, direct gauge on the paddled river corridor. Regional, neighboring-river, historical-only, lake-level, and downstream-proxy gauges do not qualify. A candidate also needs a defensible paddling threshold in the live gauge unit and public put-in/take-out evidence.

Primary inventories:

- [Minnesota DNR river levels](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/river_levels/index.html)
- [Minnesota DNR recommended paddling sections](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/state-water-trails/recommended-paddling.html)
- [USGS Water Data for Minnesota](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/mn/nwis/rt)

## P0 — next implementation candidates

1. **Cloquet River: Highway 53 Wayside to a public Burnett-area take-out** — DNR site `38` is current, reports discharge, and has official paddling bands. Blocked only on confirming a legal take-out that makes the gauge part of the route; the gauge bridge itself must not be assumed to be public access.
2. **St. Louis River: County Road 29 to Paupores** — DNR site `338` is current and lies at Floodwood inside the combined corridor. Existing adjacent access and route research can be reused.
3. **Vermilion River: County Road 24 to a lower public access bracketing site `251`** — both Buyck site `342` and the lower-river site `251` are current with official DNR bands. Avoid Crane Lake or other lake-only endpoints.
4. **Red Lake River: Sportsman's Park toward Crookston, split at a verified intermediate public access** — sites `180` and `172` are current. The unsplit route is too long for a normal day card.
5. **Big Fork River: Big Falls-area short splits** — site `5` is current and interpreted. Prefer routes that start or finish at Big Falls rather than applying it to distant downstream reaches.
6. **Little Fork River: gauge-centered short splits at Linden Grove, Silverdale, and Littlefork** — sites `88`, `295`, `294`, and `81` are current. Add only when an official intermediate access creates a useful non-duplicate day length.

## P1 — live gauges, threshold research still required

1. **Clearwater River** — USGS `05078000` at Plummer and `05078500` at Red Lake Falls both return current discharge and stage. Find paddling-specific bands and exact public access pairs.
2. **Thief River** — USGS `05076000` returns current discharge and stage. Research dam constraints, public access, and runnable bands before creating a route.
3. **Roseau River** — USGS `05107500` at Ross is current. Confirm a river-only public-access corridor and paddling thresholds.
4. **Ball Club River** — USGS `05208010` is current. Research legal access and whether the small-river flow has a documented recreational relationship.
5. **Prairie River** — USGS `05212700` near Taconite is current. Research access continuity, obstructions, and paddling thresholds.
6. **Rapid River** — USGS `05134200` near Baudette is current. Research public access, navigability, and a route-specific flow model.

## P2 — current telemetry but conflicts with current product scope

- **Kawishiwi and South Kawishiwi rivers** — USGS sites `05124480`, `05125000`, and `05126210` are current, but likely itineraries include substantial lake travel and Boundary Waters permit/logistics complexity.
- **Basswood River** — USGS `05127500` is current, but practical itineraries are lake-and-portage expeditions rather than river-only day routes.
- **Upper Mississippi headwaters around site `341`** — site `341` is Stump Lake stage and is not a qualifying river gauge. Do not republish the affected river cards under that station.

## Rejected until a new direct gauge exists

- Devil Track River
- Cascade River
- Kadunce River
- Big Sucker Creek
- Upper Brule routes above the direct Highway 61 gauge corridor
- Upper Pigeon River above the Middle Falls gauge corridor
- Lake Superior coastal routes

These may retain research records and geometry, but they must stay outside the published route catalog.
